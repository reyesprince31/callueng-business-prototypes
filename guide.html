What to bring to your first project meeting.

You do not need every answer. Bring the information you have and the questions you need to resolve.

Make it your own.

Plan my build

Start with how the place will be used.

Describe who the space is for, what it needs to support, and what matters most to you. A few clear priorities are more useful than an unfiltered list of every possible feature.

Bring the information you already have.

Location, approximate area, available drawings, photographs, and site information help the team understand where the discussion should begin.

Talk about decisions and assumptions.

Explain your budget preference and timing. Ask what is known, what still needs verification, and what must be decided before a scope or estimate can be reviewed.

Your first-meeting checklist.
