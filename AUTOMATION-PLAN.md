# A clearer path from interest to project

The differentiator is a client who arrives with a **shared visual and written brief**. The website should carry that context into the team's work rather than restarting the conversation on each channel.

## Working in this prototype

1. A prospect explores four AI concept directions and chooses exterior/interior references independently.
2. Colour preferences, everyday priorities, and the latest six assistant notes stay with the tab-session draft.
3. The local assistant recognizes design, budget, plan-set, printing, meeting, and site-support intents and routes them to the appropriate page.
4. A meeting pack embeds both preferred images and records project context, meeting focus, preferred date, and discussion questions. A separate Build Brief retains the visual preferences.
5. Plan preparation belongs to the construction brief. Blueprinting is an independent business site with local PDF selection, revision/output preferences, pages × sets, and a print-request download. Hardware/Rentals/Hauling is another independent site, with one-service or combined direct requests. Neither requires a construction enquiry.

These are local, reversible preparation steps. They do not create a live lead, send a message, reserve a meeting, produce a reviewed estimate, order supplies, or release work.

## Proposed live workflow

| Trigger | Automation | Review / decision | Useful result |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Visitor saves a direction and submits an enquiry | Create one lead record with chosen images, priorities, location, and service intent; draft a concise summary | Client reviews what will be submitted; coordinator checks scope and assigns the next step | Less repeated intake |
| A consultation is requested | Suggest appropriate meeting type, check connected availability, draft an agenda, and attach the visual brief | Staff/client confirm the appointment | A meeting with context |
| Drawings arrive for a design/build proposal | Index approved revisions; flag missing information; draft a scope checklist and questions | Relevant professionals review scope, quantities, specifications, and pricing | A more organized estimating conversation |
| A printing request arrives in the Blueprinting inbox | Parse PDFs for sheet count and page dimensions; compare requested output; identify missing specifications and organise revision references | Printing staff confirm file suitability, scale, price, finishing, and turnaround; client confirms before production | A print-job record and reviewed quote |
| A hardware/rental/hauling request arrives in its own inbox | Structure the material, equipment, or transport need; draft an availability/quotation checklist | This provider confirms specifications, equipment/stock, access, terms, and quotation | A direct service request, independent of construction |
| Approved site notes/photos are collected | Draft a client update summarizing recorded progress, open questions, and next decisions | Project manager reviews and releases the update | Less update-writing effort |
| A change is proposed | Draft the changed scope and link affected drawings/selections; flag missing cost or timing information | Responsible people review and approve through the agreed procedure | A traceable change record |
| A project approaches handover | Assemble the agreed document register and identify missing records | Team verifies completion records and follow-up items | A useful handover pack |

AI should draft, organize, and flag questions. Professional design, technical assessment, reviewed quantities/pricing, appointments, orders, and work approvals need the relevant accountable people.

## A sensible rollout

**First:** approve the separate businesses' names, branding, contacts, service scope, and responsible operators. Keep a separate lead/order queue, quotation, customer communication, and permissions for each business. Add consented enquiry delivery to the correct inbox. Cross-site links are referrals; records should not be silently shared between businesses. Replace construction role concepts with approved staff content.

**Next:** connect secure server-side AI for free-form chat and bespoke concept generation. Preserve the exact user brief, show assumptions, and version the resulting concepts. Keep API credentials on the server; apply access controls, usage limits, and the agreed retention policy to real customer records.

**Then:** connect team workflows for meeting agendas, reviewed update drafts, supplier request routing, and document registers. Add secure project permissions and an auditable review/release record before linking the client portal to real data.

The existing separate team-app prototype is a presentation reference. It is not connected to these website records yet.

## Blueprinting rollout

Begin with secure PDF upload and a reviewed quote, rather than inventing instant prices. Confirm accepted files, machine-supported sizes, paper/finishing, B&W/colour rates, minimum charges, collection, delivery coverage, and turnaround rules. Then add deterministic PDF page/dimension checks and a reviewed rate table for automated quotes. Scale and drawing revisions must remain explicit; AI must not modify drawings or approve engineering content.

For regular architects and engineers, a later print portal can store approved job references, distinguish revisions, repeat the same configuration, show job status, and support firm users under agreed access and billing terms. Reprints must identify the actual approved file/version. These features are proposed, not implemented or attributed to the current business.

## Measure the benefit

Track visual-brief completion, enquiry-to-meeting conversion, missing-information follow-ups, preparation time per meeting, reviewed-proposal turnaround, and update-release time. Compare these with an agreed baseline; no efficiency or revenue percentage is assumed in this proposal.
