Define the briefDesign the scopePrepare & buildReview & hand over
01

Define the brief

Start by understanding the place you want to create and the site you have to work with.

You bring

Location, intended use, rough area, priorities, and the information you already have.

The team clarifies

What is known, what needs verification, and what the next discussion should resolve.

02

Design the scope

Turn the brief into project information that can be reviewed, coordinated, and priced.

You review

Layout, specifications, key selections, and the assumptions in the proposed scope.

Keep a record

Drawing revisions, open decisions, proposed costs, and what is excluded.

03

Prepare & build

Resolve preparation requirements, then coordinate the agreed work with visible review points.

Before work moves ahead

Confirm documentation, the work scope, responsibilities, and dependencies.

During the build

Review approved updates, inspection records, selections, and proposed changes.

04

Review & hand over

Inspect the completed work, address agreed items, and collect the project records.

You review

The completed work and any items recorded for follow-up.

The record to keep

Relevant approved drawings, warranties, maintenance information, and agreed completion records.

What makes a timeline credible?

It identifies approvals, selections, procurement, inspections, and site conditions that the work depends on.

Read the planning guide

What happens when the scope changes?

Review the revised work, cost, and schedule impact before the change is approved and released.
